- Openness to experience is one of the core personality traits recognized in psychology, describing a person's tendency to seek out and appreciate new ideas, emotions, perspectives, and ways of engaging with the world. While some people naturally lean toward novelty and curiosity, others feel more at home with the familiar — and both are completely valid ways of being. What research increasingly suggests, though, is that gently cultivating greater openness may support emotional well-being and mental flexibility across a person's lifetime.
- One way many people explore openness is through what psychologists call "experiential engagement" — intentionally trying something new, even in small ways. This might mean reading about an unfamiliar topic, spending time in a different environment, or simply allowing yourself to sit with an emotion rather than pushing it away. Many people find that small acts of curiosity can gradually soften rigid thinking patterns that sometimes contribute to [stress]([[Understanding stress]]) or [low mood]([[Understanding low mood]]).
- Another avenue worth exploring is creative expression. Research suggests that engaging with art, music, writing, or other creative outlets — whether as a creator or an audience — may help people access and process emotions that are otherwise difficult to name. This kind of emotional exploration is linked to greater self-awareness, which is a meaningful foundation for mental health.
- Mindfulness practices may also support openness. Approaches rooted in mindfulness encourage people to notice their thoughts and feelings with curiosity rather than judgment, making it a little easier over time to sit with uncertainty or discomfort without becoming overwhelmed.
- It is worth remembering that developing any new quality takes time, and nobody has to navigate this alone. If you are finding it difficult to engage with life in ways that feel meaningful, speaking with a mental health professional can be a helpful first step. Support is available, and change — even gradual change — is genuinely possible.
